01 · Behind Aawazein

Aawazein

All of us have countless voices within us — guiding us.

Sometimes you're a kid. Sometimes a professional, a rockstar, a chef, a jury member, a judge, a protestor — and so much more.

Some voices are loud. Others are barely heard. Each is part of who we are.

Acknowledge those voices. Embrace them!

Aawazein — Some voices that we choose. And some that choose us.

02 · Behind Musikgiri

Musikgiri

Where Music meets Soul.

Music isn't just something we make or listen to. It's something we feel, live, and discover in the things we do.

A melody can mean something different to everyone. A rhythm can live in a thought, a moment, a memory, or anything we do.

There is no single way to experience music. There is only your way.

Don't just sing or listen. Live the music.

Verse 1 · Reprise

आवाज़ें कुछ शोर की हैं ख़ामोशी की कुछ आवाज़ें आवाज़ें अल्फ़ाज़ों में तो कहती हैं बिन बोले भी कुछ आवाज़ें आवाज़ें

The opening thought returns: even silence has a voice, and expression is not limited to what we say aloud.

Verse 2

कुछ मनगढ़ंत सी आवाज़ें कुछ सच्ची सी भी आवाज़ें कुछ बाहर की और कुछ अंदर की भी कुछ मनगढ़ंत सी कुछ सच्ची सी भी कुछ बाहर की और कुछ अंदर की भी

Some voices are imagined, others are real. Some come from the world around us; others rise from within. Both shape how we experience life.

Verse 3

मुझसे ये तुझ तक पहुँचे तो मक़सद है ये आवाज़ें ख़ुद मैं ही ना सुन पाऊँ तो, बेमतलब की ये आवाज़ें

A voice finds purpose when it reaches another person. But if I cannot hear my own inner voice, what meaning does it hold?

Verse 4

कुछ मनगढ़ंत सी आवाज़ें कुछ सच्ची सी भी आवाज़ कुछ बाहर की और कुछ अंदर की भी

The song revisits its central contrast: imagined and true, outside and inside. All are part of our inner conversation.

Bridge

जो कह ना सके थे हम कभी वो लफ़्ज़ हवा में रह गए तू पास आया, समझ लिया जो राज़ आँखों में बह गए

Words we could never say seemed to linger in the air. Someone close enough understood the secrets our eyes had already revealed.

Final Chorus

दिल से ये दिल तक पहुँचे तो फिर धड़कन है ये आवाज़ें आवाज़ें आवाज़ों की इस दुनिया में पहचान है कुछ ये आवाज़ें

When voices travel from one heart to another, they become a heartbeat — a way of recognizing ourselves amid a world of voices.

Vibrant Outro

कुछ मनगढ़ंत सी आवाज़ें कुछ सच्ची सी भी आवाज़ कुछ बाहर की और कुछ अंदर की भी आवाज़ें, आवाज़ें सुन kehti kya hai..ye आवाज़ें, आवाज़ें आवाज़ें… Kho ja Tu.. Musikgiri Main.. (Aawazein)

Listen to what these voices are trying to say. The outro carries that invitation into Musikgiri: lose yourself in music and in the voices that make you who you are.
