Musikgiri Website V5 — Platform logos in the header

Open index.html locally, or deploy the whole folder to a static web host.

Five clickable platform logos (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Music, Spotify,
Apple Music) appear at the TOP right on desktop and in a compact row below
navigation on mobile. The same icons appear in the footer. No extra text labels
are displayed, but all icons have accessible names and tooltips.

Change all platform URLs in config.js -> socials.
Instagram and Facebook currently lead to platform homepages until you supply
profile URLs. YouTube points to the supplied YouTube Music track, not a channel.
The release cards have their own URLs under releases[].

Aawazein cover art and its full vertical 9:16 experience poster remain separate.
The collaboration email is mymusikgiri@gmail.com.
